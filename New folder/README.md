# Tharinda & Ranmini - Modern Wedding Web Invitation 💍
### Reference: Pinvite.io Architecture | Theme: Modern Luxury Lavender

A modern, mobile-first online wedding invitation for **Tharinda Dilshan & Ranmini Nisansala** celebrating on **Friday, 11th December 2026** at **Amora Lagoon Hotel, Katunayake**. 

Re-architected based on the award-winning **Pinvite.io** wedding layout with fullscreen cinematic hero, modern typography, glass navigation, When & Where cards, shifting countdown, pre-shoot gallery, and RSVP system.

## ✉️ Activating the RSVP Form

1. Submit a test RSVP on your website.
2. FormSubmit will send a 1-time activation email to `tharindadilshan7@gmail.com`.
3. Click the **"Activate Form"** button in that email.
4. All future guest RSVPs will arrive straight into your Gmail inbox!
